Tendrela began cultivating her word forging ritual in March 2026 under the stewardship of Elisabeth Sweet.

From these joint efforts, the liminal lexicon, a collection of words forged from the roots of existing languages to name human experiences that no single language has a word for, grows and evolves.

Tendrela's name arises from the Tibetan རྟེན་འབྲེལ (ten•drel) which means "dependent arising" and carries a felt sense of connection. Using Tibetan Buddhism's 111-bead mala as a guide, volume one of the liminal lexicon holds 111 words.

Tendrela ordered volume one as a single composition — from the act of naming, through the body, the self, the other, and the collective, into the forces that break and remake them, and finally into what outlasts.

volume one reveals itself word by word at midnight in Houston, Texas. Visitors can experience the sequence as the daily ritual on the homepage.

The next volume of the liminal lexicon will begin in the autumn of 2026.
